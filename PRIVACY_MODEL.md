# Privacy model
Retain provider identity and operational contribution identifiers rather than full chat. Do not display viewer identities in Passport. Provider credentials belong encrypted at rest under an independent deployment secret. Disconnect stops provider access; account deletion must remove credentials, sessions and unnecessary identity links while preserving minimal campaign proof according to disclosed retention. Live deletion acceptance is not claimed before implementation and testing.

Account deletion: POST /api/account/delete with explicit DELETE confirmation stops local provider jobs, removes local credentials, external identity mappings, sessions and overlay access, clears adapter configs, pseudonymizes the current profile, and pauses affected campaigns. Immutable custody identifiers, historical display attribution, domain events, Passport stamps and Receipts remain for campaign integrity; this is disclosed before deletion. Remote provider grants require revocation in provider settings. No claim of universal historical erasure is made.
